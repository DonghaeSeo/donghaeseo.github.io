# Website assets

Profile portrait and email-free public curriculum vitae for Donghae Seo's academic website.

Institutional emblems are copied unmodified from the official KAIST and POSTECH
identity resources:

- KAIST: https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/html/kaist/011901.html
- POSTECH: https://www.postech.ac.kr/kor/university-introduction/emblem.do

STIX Two Text WOFF2 files come from the official STIX v2.0.2 archive:
https://github.com/stipub/stixfonts/tree/master/archive/STIXv2.0.2/WOFF2
The SIL Open Font License is included at `fonts/OFL.txt`.

The current portrait is a 4:5 crop of the owner's first San Francisco photo
from 2026, showing a front-facing pose. On October 18, 2026, the owner selected
candidate 2 from the Google Scholar profile crops, then requested a vertical
rectangle with the same face scale. The 1600 × 2000 PNG preserves that crop's
horizontal bounds and adds 140 pixels above and 260 below from the original.
After EXIF orientation, its crop rectangle is x=790, y=1500, width=1600,
height=2000. It is an original photo crop without AI retouching. The website
displays its full frame.
