Local map assets for the personal homepage

Load in order:
1. d3.v7.9.0.min.js -> window.d3
2. topojson-client.v3.1.0.min.js -> window.topojson
3. land-50m.js -> window.AFFILIATION_LAND (GeoJSON FeatureCollection)
4. land-110m.js -> window.AFFILIATION_LAND_WORLD (GeoJSON FeatureCollection)

All assets are local classic scripts: no network or file:// fetch required.
Use d3.geoEqualEarth() for the Equal Earth map and d3.geoOrthographic()
for a globe. Land at 1:50 million is intended for regional close-ups;
it is not a street-level campus map.
Land at 1:110 million is included for faster world/globe rendering.

World-atlas land comes from Natural Earth. Natural Earth data are public
 domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
The wrapper retains the original TopoJSON data and decodes it at runtime.
No country borders or labels are included.

Visit-marker data and institution-specific official source URLs are stored in
../../locations-data.js. Official pages establish the campus city/area; the
rounded visit coordinates are approximate map positions, not numerical
coordinates quoted from those pages. Cambridge uses West Cambridge broadly,
without claiming which Cavendish building was visited. Kagoshima uses the
Korimoto campus area, without claiming a specific conference venue.
No visit purposes or participation dates are included.
