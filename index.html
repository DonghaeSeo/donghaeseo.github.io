Research

All publications

Quantum Hall hierarchy and anyon superconductivity

I study how anyon condensation connects quantum Hall phases and unconventional superconductors, using categorical methods, effective field theories, and explicit wave functions.

  • Wavefunctions for Anyon Superconductors arXiv:2609.04187
  • Superconductivity proximate to non-Abelian fractional spin Hall insulator in twisted bilayer MoTe2 Nature Communications 17, 7005 (2026)
  • U(2) Chern-Simons-Ginzburg-Landau theory of fractional quantum Hall hierarchies Accepted as a Letter in Physical Review B
  • A Unified Categorical Description of Quantum Hall Hierarchy and Anyon Superconductivity arXiv:2602.03848

Topological data and physical constraints

I classify anyon statistics and study what they imply for chiral edge transport, intrinsic sign problems, and microscopic realizations of topological phases.

  • Most Two-Dimensional Bosonic Topological Orders Forbid Sign-Problem-Free Quantum Monte Carlo Simulation: Nonpositive Gauss Sum as an Indicator Physical Review Letters 135, 146602 (2025)
  • Noninvertible duality and symmetry topological order of one-dimensional lattice models with spatially modulated symmetry Physical Review B 111, 115112 (2025)
  • Modular extension of topological orders from congruence representations Physical Review B 109, 155105 (2024)
  • Classification of fermionic topological orders from congruence representations Physical Review B 108, 115103 (2023)
